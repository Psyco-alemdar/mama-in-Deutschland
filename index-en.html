FAQ

Before you start

Is it free?

Current access is free. Registration does not start a paid subscription or automatic payment.

Can I try it before registering?

Yes. The French-language demo includes an interactive checklist and a budget calculator. Demo examples are not saved to an account.

Which languages are available?

These public pages are available in French, German, English and Arabic. Some personal pages are only partly translated; Turkish, Russian and Ukrainian are not complete.

Is this a medical service?

No. Mama helps with organisation and understanding. General information does not replace advice from your midwife or doctor.

Are applications submitted automatically?

No. The tools help you prepare. You submit applications through the official organisations.

How does the community work?

Sharing spaces require an account. Avoid identifiable health information and report problematic content.
